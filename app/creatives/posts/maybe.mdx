publishedAt: '2026-02-07'
summary: 'A small poem about possibility, patience, and Valentine’s Day.'

maybe you'll be mine,
maybe this valentine,
maybe we're really meant to be,
maybe... we'll just have to wait and see.

maybe i'll be yours,
now or maybe in years,
maybe you'll be mine,
maybe it'll be this valentine.
